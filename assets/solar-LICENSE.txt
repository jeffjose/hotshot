The icons in icons.ttf are from Solar, by 480 Design, in its "linear"
style, as published by Iconify (@iconify-json/solar).

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made here: the line is drawn 2.25 wide where Solar draws it at
1.5, the lines are turned into filled outlines, and the icons hotshot
uses are gathered into a font (tools/icon_font.py). The line, rectangle,
ellipse, counter, blur and filled-rectangle icons are not Solar's: they
are drawn in that script, in Solar's manner.
